# Stoa challenge: Make Blueprints Talk (Junction 2026)
Build a program that answers natural-language questions about construction drawings and building information models (BIM) accurately and verifiably. A great answer is correct AND shows where in the drawing it came from, so a human can verify it in seconds.
Dataset: real technical drawings in many formats and languages, plus example questions.
- preview (0.5 GB): https://fwjydgxpttajiqpfmqtj.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/stoa-junction-2026/junction-dataset-preview.zip
- full (10 GB): https://fwjydgxpttajiqpfmqtj.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/stoa-junction-2026/junction-dataset.zip
Stoa wants solutions next year's frontier models won't make obsolete: hybrids of frontier models + computer vision, 3D, geometry, small fine-tuned models.
Judging (25% each): accuracy; visualizing reasoning so a human can verify; creativity; LOW inference cost.
Prizes: 1st 1000 EUR + work trial at Stoa; 2nd 750 EUR; 3rd 250 EUR.
Final submission: Sunday Nov 15, 10:00 Helsinki.
